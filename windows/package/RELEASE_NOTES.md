**Win.apk 1.0 beta.** Win.apk runs Android apps directly on your Windows 10/11 (x64) PC, as ordinary Windows programs: each app gets its own window, Start menu entry and notifications, with no emulator and no virtual machine. It is an unofficial port of [Mac.apk](https://github.com/kksimp/Mac.apk).

> Not code-signed yet: SmartScreen may warn. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Download
- **`Win.apk-Setup.exe`**: the installer (per user, no administrator rights). Recommended.
- **`Win.apk-windows-x64.zip`**: portable; unzip it and run `Win.apk.exe`.

## What it does
- **Apps as on a phone.** Apps run on Android 14's own app, view and graphics code, drawn on your graphics card.
- **A built-in Store.** Search Google Play's catalogue and install free apps from inside Win.apk.
- **Install any app file.** `.apk`, `.apkm`, `.xapk` and `.apks`, by double-click or drag and drop.
- **ARM apps too.** Apps that ship only 64-bit ARM code are translated for your PC.
- **Your PC's hardware.** Webcam, microphone, location, Bluetooth, game controllers and your Windows folders.
- **Google services (experimental).** Google Play services and the Play Store, with a guided setup.
- English and Hebrew, light and dark.

## Limitations
- Apps with only 32-bit ARM (`armeabi-v7a`) code do not run.
- No in-app purchases, Play Integrity or Vulkan; no sensors.
