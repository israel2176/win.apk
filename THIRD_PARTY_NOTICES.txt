==============================

Win.apk is distributed as Win.apk-Setup.exe and Win.apk-windows-x64.zip. Both
contain the third-party components listed below, each used under its own
licence, which is reproduced here or shipped beside the component as named.
Win.apk's own code is licensed under the GNU AGPL v3.0 (LICENSE.txt).

The Android apps you choose to run with Win.apk are not part of Win.apk and are
not covered by these notices; you are responsible for your rights to them.

The source tree also holds files of upstream Mac.apk that the Windows build
does not use (its macOS libraries and their notices); Mac.apk's own notices are
in its repository, https://github.com/kksimp/Mac.apk .

4. Components used at run time but not shipped
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- ICU: Windows' own icu.dll (Windows 10 1903 and later).
- The Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, Media Foundation, WASAPI and the other
  Windows APIs: part of Windows.
- F-Droid's catalog (index-v1.json) and app icons: downloaded from
  https://f-droid.org when the Store opens. The apps, their names,
  descriptions and icons belong to their authors.
- Google's Play services, Services Framework and Play Store: never shipped or
  downloaded by Win.apk; installed by the user from Google-signed files.
